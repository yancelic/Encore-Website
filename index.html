Installing Encore on macOS

Open the DMG and drag Encore into Applications. Quit Encore and its background recorder before replacing an older installation. Updates are installed manually.

The app is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. After the first launch attempt, you may need System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

In Encore, open Settings → Permissions. Switch on Encore for screen recording and microphone, and Encore Recorder for Input Monitoring. Follow the illustrated guide for each step.

Read the installation notes

What should I expect from the Linux beta?

Ubuntu and Flatpak are development previews with manual updates. Desktop integration, Wayland permissions, GPU drivers, audio routing, and playback may still need fixes. The Flatpak download is a standalone bundle, not a Flathub listing.

Follow the Linux beta test guide for installation steps, checks, and log locations.

A few things to know.

How does saving a replay work?

Encore records into a rolling buffer. Use the save hotkey, Alt+S by default, to keep the recent gameplay. Choose a buffer from 15 to 120 seconds in the desktop app.

Can I edit game, chat, and microphone audio separately?

Yes. Encore captures three separate audio tracks, with per-track waveforms, volume controls, and mute toggles in the clip editor.

What can I export?

Trim a clip, export a 9:16 vertical video, or combine several clips into a montage. You can also copy clip files straight to the Windows clipboard.

Is Encore open source?

Yes. Encore is licensed under GPL-3.0. You can inspect the code, report issues, and find setup instructions on GitHub.
